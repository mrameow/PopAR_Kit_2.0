# PopAR Kit 2.0

A fun, movement-based game that helps children learn to spell and build English vocabulary — using nothing but their hand or body in front of a webcam. No mouse, no keyboard, just play.

**Play it now:** https://mrameow.github.io/PopAR_Kit_2.0/

## Why it helps with English learning

- **Learn spelling by doing, not memorizing** — children pop bubbles or step into lanes to pick the correct letter, filling in missing letters or spelling whole words themselves.
- **Builds vocabulary with pictures** — every word can come with its own picture, helping young learners connect the word to its meaning instead of just recognizing letters.
- **Practices words in real sentences** — the Super Sentence mode asks children to complete a full sentence, so they learn how a word is actually used, not just spelled in isolation.
- **Instant feedback keeps learning on track** — correct and incorrect answers are shown right away with clear colors, messages, and sound, so children immediately know what to fix.
- **Active learning through movement** — pointing, popping, walking, and dodging keep children physically engaged, which helps hold attention far better than tapping a screen.
- **Two ways to play, so every child finds their pace**
  - 🫧 **Point and Pop** — pop the correct floating bubble with a finger.
  - 🚶 **Stay in Lane** — walk into the correct lane with the whole body while dodging obstacles, great for classroom or group play.
- **Adjustable difficulty** — Easy mode (fill in one missing letter) and Hard mode (spell the whole word) let the same word list stretch across different skill levels.
- **Teachers and parents can create their own word lists** — add your own words, pictures, and sentences to match any lesson, spelling list, or topic your child is learning.
- **Works fully offline** — once installed, no internet connection is needed, so it can be used anywhere, including classrooms with no Wi-Fi.

## How to Play

1. Open the [live site](https://mrameow.github.io/PopAR_Kit_2.0/) and allow camera access.
2. Choose a game mode (**Point and Pop** or **Stay in Lane**) and a word list, sentence list, or level.
3. Follow the 3-2-1 countdown, then use hand or body movement to answer each question.
4. Correct and incorrect answers are shown instantly with color feedback and sound.

## Installing for Offline Play

PopAR Kit 2.0 can be installed like an app on a phone, tablet, or computer, so it can be played anywhere without an internet connection — perfect for classrooms or homes with limited Wi-Fi.
